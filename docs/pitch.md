# 1-minute pitch

**Problem (15 s).** Under the EHDS, every EU country needs a Health Data Access Body that decides who may reuse health data. Today that means lawyers reading every request and analysts computing numbers by hand. That is slow, and simple tricks still leak patients: ask "women with breast cancer in Austria", then "Austria without Burgenland", subtract, and you know about the two women in Burgenland.

**Solution (30 s).** Health Data Navigator turns this into one checked flow. The applicant fills in a simple form. Our **Guardian** agent checks the request against the law, with AI and fixed rules side by side, and the stricter one wins. It remembers everything already released, so subtraction tricks are blocked. Approved requests get privacy-protected statistics in seconds, or a secure workspace with no download at all. Our **Evidence Investigator** tells researchers what the numbers can and cannot prove. Staff see every step, decide appeals, and every decision lands in a tamper-evident audit log.

**Proof and ask (15 s).** On the 10 official requests: 10 of 10 decisions, all numbers correct, zero privacy leaks, under two seconds each. Faster access for research, stronger protection for patients. That's Health Data Navigator.
