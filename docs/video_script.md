# 2-minute demo video script

About 1:58. One story: problem, solution, four demo moments, results, roadmap, team. Demo screens are dimmed except the highlighted part.

| # | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | EU flag and title. Then two cards: a pile of requests, an hourglass ("slow and rigid"). | Europe is opening its health data for research. For every request, a Health Data Access Body must decide who may use the data, for what, and how patients stay anonymous. Manual review is slow, and fixed rules cannot foresee every case. |
| 2 | "What can go wrong?" Three drawn cards appear with the words: insurer briefcase with rising € (misuse), magnifier on one person in a crowd (re-identification), masked thief with a folder (data theft). | Three things can go wrong. Data misused against patients, for example by an insurer. Numbers so small that a person can be recognised. Or records being stolen. |
| 3 | Five step cards light up one by one: simple form, Guardian check, protected answer, Evidence Investigator, trace for staff. | Health Data Navigator works in five steps: a simple form, a Guardian check, a protected answer, an Evidence Investigator that explains it, and a full trace for staff. |
| 4 | Demo 1 (R08). Spotlight on the purpose, then "Approved" with the articles, a "<10" cell, and the Evidence Investigator note. | A research group asks for the average BMI of women with breast cancer, per region. Approved in seconds, with the legal basis. Groups under ten patients are hidden, and the Evidence Investigator notes what the numbers cannot prove. |
| 5 | Demo 2 (R02). Spotlight on the insurer purpose, "Rejected · Art. 54", the letter, and the "Request manual review" button. | An insurer wants to set premiums. Rejected: the law forbids it. The AI can only make decisions stricter, never approve. Applicants can still ask for a human review. |
| 6 | Equation: Austria 114 − without Burgenland 112 = 2. Then the real D11 result, spotlight "Held back: would reveal 2 women". | Here is a trick that simple rules miss. Both numbers look harmless. But subtract them, and you learn about two women in Burgenland. Our Guardian remembers what each applicant already received, and holds the second number back. |
| 7 | Demo 4 (D09). Spotlight on "Records in a secure workspace", the workspace, and the "Download is not possible" notice. | Researchers who truly need records get a secure workspace. They analyse the data inside, but nothing can be downloaded. Only summary results leave. |
| 8 | Counters: 10/10 decisions, 6/6 numbers, 0 privacy leaks. Line: "AI sees the request and released numbers · patient records stay inside". | On all ten official test requests, every decision and number was correct, with zero privacy leaks. The AI judges only the request and numbers already released. Patient records stay inside. |
| 9 | "Today's demo. Tomorrow's platform.": EHDS Navigator in the middle; Guardian and Evidence Investigator marked TODAY; six PLANNED agents appear around it (Research, Cohorts, Discovery, Literature, Federation, Collaboration). Line: "Secure workspace, planned: SAS · R · Python · PSPP". | This prototype is built as a platform. Today: Guardian and Evidence Investigator. Next: agents for study design, cohorts, datasets, literature, cross-border analysis and collaboration, plus SAS, R, Python and PSPP. |
| 10 | "The team": photos with names (Christian Hörantner, Axel Kalinowski, Laura Dobler). Photos are not stored in the repo. | Built by Christian, Axel and Laura. |
