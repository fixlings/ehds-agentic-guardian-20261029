# 2-minute demo video script (v2)

About 1:58. One story: problem, solution, four demo moments, results, roadmap. Screens are dimmed except the highlighted part.

| # | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Animated: EU star ring, "European Health Data Space". Then: piles of request letters, hourglass "slow, manual work". | Europe is opening its health data for research. A Health Data Access Body must decide on every request: who may use the data, for what, and how patients stay anonymous. Today, that is slow, manual work. |
| 2 | "What can go wrong?" Three picture cards pop in with the words: businessman with money bag (misuse), magnifying glass on a person (re-identification), thief with a folder (data theft). | Three things can go wrong. Data misused against patients, for example by an insurer. Numbers so small that a person can be recognised. Or records being stolen. |
| 3 | "Health Data Navigator, prototype": four boxes (form, Guardian, lock, staff) light up one by one as they are named. | Our prototype, Health Data Navigator, works in four steps. A simple form. A Guardian check. A protected answer. And staff can trace every decision. |
| 4 | Demo 1 (R08). Spotlight on the purpose field, click Check, spotlight on "Approved" with the articles, spotlight on a "<10" cell: "Fewer than 10 patients → hidden". | A research group asks for the average BMI of women with breast cancer, per region. Approved in seconds, with the legal basis and a plain letter. Groups with fewer than ten patients are hidden. |
| 5 | Demo 2 (R02). Spotlight on the insurer purpose, then "Rejected · Art. 54", the letter ("AI can only be stricter"), and the "Request manual review" button. | An insurer wants to set premiums. Rejected: the law forbids it. The AI can only make decisions stricter, never approve. And a human reviews every appeal. |
| 6 | Animated equation: Austria 114 − Austria without Burgenland 112 = 2 women 🚨. Fade to the real D11 result, spotlight "Held back: would reveal 2 women". | Here is a trick that simple rules miss. Both numbers look harmless. But subtract them, and you learn about two women in Burgenland. Our Guardian remembers what each applicant already received, and holds the second number back. |
| 7 | Demo 4 (D09). Spotlight on "Records in a secure workspace" (with reason), then on the workspace and the "Download is not possible" notice. | Researchers who truly need records get a secure workspace. They analyse the data inside, but nothing can be downloaded. Only summary results leave. |
| 8 | Animated counters: 10/10 decisions, 6/6 numbers, 0 privacy leaks. Line: "Patient data never goes to the AI · works without AI". | On all ten official test requests, every decision and every number was correct, with zero privacy leaks. Patient data never goes to the AI, and the system also works without it. |
| 9 | "Prototype → Platform": the Navigator in the middle, modules pop in around it (SAS, R, Python, PSPP, user logins, new modules where demand shows). End card: "Faster research. Safer patients." | This is a prototype, built as a platform. Next: SAS, R, Python and PSPP inside the workspace, user logins, and new modules wherever demand shows. Health Data Navigator: faster research, safer patients. |
